## PhoneBox Deck 1.2.7

- WoW action folders use the available space in both orientations. Party & Raid shows two rows of three in landscape and three rows of two in portrait at the Vizio’s screen size, with larger icons and labels.
- The Windows web preview and Android app use the same layout. The full 17-key numpad keeps its standard arrangement.
- New Windows setups include the Party & Raid folder. Existing layouts are preserved, and the synced party actions can be added from **WoW Actions → Choose actions**.
- Includes PhoneBox Spellbook 1.2.4, which adds six Party & Raid actions and opens WoW Forever’s Looking For Group window directly.
