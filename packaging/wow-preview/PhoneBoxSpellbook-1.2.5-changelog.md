## PhoneBox Spellbook 1.2.5 — WoW Forever 1.60.1

- Fixed crafted **Camp Tent** missing from PhoneBox’s synced actions. Spellbook now detects the tent in your bags by item ID, including when WoW hasn’t loaded its use-spell data.
- Refreshes the add-on’s action list after bag contents change.

After updating, restart WoW. With a Camp Tent in your bags, run `/pbspells` outside combat to sync it. In PhoneBox Deck 1.2.8, find it under **Camping → Camp Tent** in the action picker.
