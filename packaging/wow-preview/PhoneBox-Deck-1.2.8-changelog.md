## PhoneBox Deck 1.2.8

- Added category and action dropdowns to the WoW button editor on Android and in Windows Settings. The multi-action checklist remains available.
- Camping items, Party & Raid add-on actions, and personal WoW macros each have their own category.
- New setups include a **Camping** folder with **Tent** linked to the crafted **Camp Tent**. Existing layouts can link it through **Camping → Camp Tent** in the action picker.
- Includes PhoneBox Spellbook 1.2.5 for WoW Forever 1.60.1.
