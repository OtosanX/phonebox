## PhoneBox Spellbook 1.2.4

- Added Party & Raid actions for Next Enemy, Ready Check, Group Finder, Skull Marker, Cross Marker, and Clear Marker.
- Group Finder opens WoW Forever’s Looking For Group window.
- Party & Raid actions use bindings set up by PhoneBox Spellbook. WoW still controls targeting and group permissions.

For WoW Forever 1.60.1. After updating, restart WoW and run `/pbspells` outside combat to refresh the deck’s action list.
